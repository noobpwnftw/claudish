en_US → cd_AI AN INPUT METHOD FOR AI

If you can type there,
you can use AI there.

cd_AI is an input method for Linux, Windows, and macOS. Switch to it like any keyboard language, type a few plain words, and press Tab: they come back as the prose the internet now runs on, or in a voice you pick. It uses the model you choose: Claude with your own API key, or one on your own computer.

English goes in. Em dashes come out.

01 / TWO MODES, ONE SHIFT

Compose, or continue—Shift switches.

Rewrite

Where cd_AI starts: type, and Tab shows your words in the voice you picked, corporate slop unless you say otherwise. Tab again puts it in; your words stay until then.

you typecan't make it tonight, sorry
cd_AIRegrettably, I must convey that I won't be able to make it tonight—my current bandwidth is fully allocated to high-priority deliverables, but I look forward to connecting when our schedules realign!Tab

Instruct

Type what you want and press Enter; drafts wait in the panel, and Tab takes one. It can look things up first, and ask before it acts.

you typedecline the meeting politely
cd_AIthree drafts to choose fromTab

Continue

A Shift tap, and your keys go straight in. With a local model, pause and the rest of the sentence waits after the caret.

you typeThanks for the review on
cd_AI…the parser change.Tab
02 / FIVE VOICES

Same message. Different personality.

Every voice keeps what you said: a yes stays a yes, and the names and numbers stay put. Pick one in your profile, or write your own; the words cd_AI says to a model are a file you can open.

you typepump 3 trippd agian, no injurys
corporateHeads up to the team: Pump 3 tripped again, but the critical takeaway is that we are maintaining a zero-injury environment—a testament to our unwavering commitment to safety protocols!
salesmanListen up! Pump 3 tripped AGAIN, but guess what?! NO INJURIES! This is the absolute best news you'll hear all day!
criticPump 3 has, predictably, tripped once more; at least, one must concede, no injuries have been sustained this time.
bardAlas, hear this tidings! Pump 3 hath tripped anon, yet fear not, for no injuries have befallen any soul!
proofreadPump 3 tripped again, no injuries.
03 / FOR EVERYONE WHO WRITES

Help where you write, in your own words.

No new app and no copying into a chat window: the help comes to the box you are already typing in, and nothing happens until you press a key.

Keep your words

With the proofread voice, Tab only corrects spelling and grammar, and the words stay yours: for dyslexia, a second language, or a hurried day.

you typepump 3 trippd agian, no injurys
cd_AIpump 3 tripped again, no injuriesTab

Say what you need

In compose, describe the message and press Enter; choose a draft, or press Esc for your own words back.

you typeflour 2 bags short, credit by tuesday
cd_AIIn reviewing the latest inventory metrics, it appears we are currently facing a slight deficit of two bags of flour. To proactively mitigate any potential supply chain disruptions, could we please arrange for the necessary credit to be processed by Tuesday?Tab

Ask what it means

Ask about what is in front of you. The answer is shown to read, and goes into your text only if you say so.

you typewhat does this error mean
cd_AIIt can't find a piece it needs to build.Esc
  • Nothing goes anywhere by itself. It never sends, posts, or saves for you. Every suggestion waits for your key, and Esc brings back what you typed.
  • Fewer keystrokes. A few words become a whole reply, and with a local model Tab finishes your sentences: kind to tired hands and sore wrists.
  • Your tone. Corporate slop is the default. A salesman, a critic or a bard is one setting away, proofread keeps your words, or describe your own.
  • Your model, your key. cd_AI ships no model: it uses the one you point it at, a frontier model with your own key or one on your own computer. Password fields are never touched.
04 / THE KEYS

Everything else is typing.

People who type Chinese or Japanese already know this grammar: compose, see the candidates, choose, commit. cd_AI extends it from what you meant to say to what you meant to do.

Shifttap to switch: continue or compose
Tabtake the model's text: the suggestion, a rewrite (shown first; Tab again puts it in), the highlighted draft
Enterin compose, send it as an instruction, a reply, or an answer; it never inserts the model's text
Shift+Enterin compose, keep it as it is
↑ ↓choose a draft (Tab inserts it) or an answer (Enter sends it)
F8with an instruction, also send this window
Escone step back: what you typed, then edit, then drop
Shift+Escbring back what Esc dropped, or the exchange you left
05 / BRING YOUR OWN INTELLIGENCE

The caret is model-agnostic.

  • Yours to choose. Claude with your API key (the default: it works on any computer), any OpenAI-compatible server, or an agent CLI. Hosted backends are labelled.
  • Or local. setup --profile local downloads llama.cpp and Gemma 4 E4B (4.9 GB), checksum-verified. Continue suggests at every pause.
  • Context. Files, commands, and MCP servers, per app: the PR diff in your editor, the ticket in your browser. Import the MCP servers you already use with Claude.
  • Tools and agents. An instruction may call your MCP tools, ask you a question, or hand the task to Claude Code or another agent. You approve every side effect.
  • Restraint. It runs only on what you type, sees a window only when you press F8 (and, at a terminal prompt, the terminal's last lines, unless you turn that off), and never inserts anything you did not accept. Password fields bypass it.
06 / INSTALL

Let your agent do it.

Paste this into Claude Code (or any coding agent) on the computer where you want cd_AI. It shows you the plan before it changes anything, and leaves sudo and administrator steps to you.

PROMPT
Install Claudish (cd_AI), an input method that puts a language model at the caret, on this computer.

1. Check that Python 3.10 or newer and git are installed. If not, tell me how to install them and stop. On macOS, do only this instead: clone the repository (step 2), run `python3 platforms/macos/build.py` and `python3 platforms/macos/package.py` there, open `dist/macos/cd_AI.pkg`, and tell me the installer does the rest: its last step opens cd_AI's settings, where I paste my Claude key myself; then I log out and back in.
2. Clone https://github.com/noobpwnftw/claudish into ~/claudish (or pull it if it is already there) and work in that folder. Use python3 on Linux and macOS, python on Windows.
3. Create a virtual environment there and install it with the Claude SDK: `python -m venv .venv`, then `.venv/bin/python -m pip install -e ".[claude]"` (`.venv\Scripts\python` on Windows). Use that venv's Python for every `python -m claudish` command below.
4. Run `python -m claudish setup --dry-run`, show me the plan, and ask me before continuing.
5. Run `python -m claudish setup --yes`. It writes a config, registers the input method, and starts cd_AI at login.
6. If setup says a step needs me (sudo on Linux; on Windows, cd_AI-setup.exe as administrator), show me the exact command and why. Do not run anything with sudo or as administrator yourself.
7. cd_AI ships no model: it is set up for Claude with my own API key. Run `python -m claudish settings`: it opens cd_AI's settings in my browser, where I paste the key myself (cd_AI checks it with Anthropic and keeps it where its service reads it). Wait for me. Never ask me to paste the key to you, never print it, and never write it into a file.
8. Run `python -m claudish doctor` and follow its fixes until every check passes, or explain what is left.
9. Finish by telling me to log out and back in, add "cd_AI — Claudish" in my input sources, and the keys: cd_AI starts in compose, where Tab asks for a rewrite (Tab again puts it in), Enter sends my words as an instruction, Shift+Enter keeps them as typed, and Esc steps back; tapping Shift switches to continue, where keys go straight in.

Make no mistakes.

Or by hand

git clone https://github.com/noobpwnftw/claudish
cd claudish
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/python -m pip install -e ".[claude]"
.venv/bin/python -m claudish setup
.venv/bin/python -m claudish settings

Setup asks before each step and skips what is already done; --dry-run shows the plan. Settings opens in your browser: paste your Claude key there and pick a voice. On macOS, the installer does all of this. Details in docs/install.md.

07 / WHERE IT STANDS

What is tested, and where.

macOS (IMK)Apple silicon, macOS 26 in a VM: the package installs, and after a logout "ok" + Tab rewrites in TextEdit. Not notarized: macOS asks you to open it anyway once. Intel Macs: not yet.
Windows (PIME/TSF)Windows 11 ARM64 in a VM: the installer, and after a restart "ok" + Tab rewrites in Notepad. Not signed: SmartScreen asks once. x64: not yet tested.
Linux (IBus)Preview: the one-line installer in a clean Ubuntu 24.04 container; real typing (GTK, Qt, GNOME Terminal on X11, GNOME on Wayland) last verified in containers before 2026-10-24. KDE is not yet tested.
